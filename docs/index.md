<p id="about" class="outlined blurb">
	The <b>Rodent Automated and Integrated Learning (RAIL) system</b> is an <mark>inexpensive</mark>, <mark>reliable</mark>, and <mark>scalable</mark> hardware and software system for presenting visual stimuli and providing automated reinforcements in order to train mice on touchscreen-based complex visually-guided behavioral tasks.
	<br><br>
	By leveraging the power of web development tools, <mark>open source</mark> software, and Internet of Things (<mark>IoT</mark>) hardware, mass behavioral training is easily configured, implemented, and monitored with RAIL.
